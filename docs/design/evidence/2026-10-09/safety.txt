Safety proof for the visual audit runs (hashes only; no store contents were read, printed or copied).

Method: every run copies the built app to a temp dir, sets CFBundleIdentifier to com.shoemoney.skitch-redux.eyedump-<pid>, ad-hoc re-signs it,
and launches the bundle binary with CFFIXED_USER_HOME=<temp>/home, SKITCH_APP_SUPPORT=<temp>/support-<run> (fresh each run, secrets in memory) and
SKITCH_APPEARANCE=modern. The harness refuses to run unless all three isolation conditions hold and the bundle id is not the installed app's.
